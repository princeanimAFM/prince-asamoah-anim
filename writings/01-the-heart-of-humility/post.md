# The Heart of Humility: as posted

Prince singing "In Christ Alone" (`song-in-christ-alone.m4a`).

## Cards
1. **The Heart of Humility.** The heart of a man the Lord establishes.
2. **Honour is in the Lord's hand.** Yet we are so quick to feel anger when disrespected, and so eager
   to prove how big and important we are.
3. **They never defended their honour.** David, king over all Israel, was defied and spoken against.
   Jesus was questioned again and again. Neither rose to defend himself.
4. **They knew who God made them.** So they had no desire to prove themselves, or their relevance, to
   anyone.
5. **Why didn't David fight back?** When you trust God wholly to protect what He has given you, you
   leave Him to fight for what you cannot.
6. **What looked like weakness.** (Saul · Abner · Absalom · Amasa · Shimei) Sparing them looked like
   political weakness, just as Joab saw it. Yet David came out on top of every situation, and his God
   came through for him.
7. **He chose mercy.** Despised, rebelled against, defied as king, he still chose mercy, love and the
   mind of God. Vengeance belongs to God alone.
8. **A heart like God's.** A heart that loved as God loves, repented easily, and sought God in every
   situation.
9. **A prayer.** Oh God, let me love as You love, see as You see, speak as You speak, and think Your
   thoughts.
10. **Amen.** That my life will reflect Your life. That Your love will engross my entire being and
    flood my heart!
