# The Heart of Humility

*The heart of a man the Lord establishes.*

Notes on David and Adonijah's supporters. The original handwritten pages are in this folder
(`notebook-page-1.jpg`, `notebook-page-2.jpg`).

---

It is in the Lord's hand to give honour. So often, we are eager to prove to people how big we are,
and how important they should see us.

David was a king over all of Israel, yet many people constantly defied his authority and spoke
harshly of him. Yet this man never rose to defend his honour. Why else would God identify Himself
with him?

Jesus was questioned so many times, yet He did not try or rise to defend His honour or authority.

I see that both of these men were so conscious of who God had made them that they had no desire to
prove themselves, or their relevance, to anyone.

When our pride is exposed or our ego is hurt, it is because of the shame and anger we feel at being
disrespected. We are so quick to feel anger and disgust when people treat us badly or offend us.
David understood that vengeance belonged to God alone, and through his constant trust, God kept
fighting for him and establishing him.

Why didn't David try to protect what many people would fight over today? By placing your whole trust
in God, and leaving Him alone to protect and defend what He has given you, you leave Him to fight
for what you cannot.

It is so important to see the heart of God and His mind in every situation. It was easy for everyone
to see David's inaction after the sin of Amnon as a failure, and his decision not to kill Saul,
Abner, Absalom, Amasa and Shimei as political weakness, just as Joab saw it. Yet some do not realise
that David came out on top of every situation, and his God came through for him.

How many times was he despised, rebelled against, and defied as a king? Yet he chose mercy, love and
the mind of God. His was a heart that loved as God loves, repented easily, and sought God in every
situation.

## Prayer

Oh God, let me love as You love, see as You see, speak as You speak, and think Your thoughts.
That my life will reflect Your life.
That Your love will engross my entire being and flood my heart!
