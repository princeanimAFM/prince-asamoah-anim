# What Do We Mean When We Call God Jealous?

*Planned: part two of "Is It a Sin to Be Angry, or to Experience Emotions?"*

"For thou shalt worship no other god: for the LORD, whose name is Jealous, is a jealous God."
(Exodus 34:14)

## Starting point from part one
Jealousy comes from love: a wish to protect what is ours, and a sense of wanting good for ourselves.
Yet it is easily corrupted when it puts others below itself.
