# Is It a Sin to Be Angry? As posted

**Posted:** personal TikTok, November 2026
**Format:** 15 swipeable cards (`cards/card-01.jpg` to `card-15.jpg`), with Prince's own singing as the sound.

## Cards
1. **Is it a sin to be angry, or to experience emotions?** (Some teenagers asked me) "Be ye angry, and sin not." Ephesians 4:26
2. **The question.** Some teenagers asked me a question. We see a lot of evil done from anger, hatred,
   jealousy and so on. Is it a sin to be angry, or to experience emotions? If not, what is the purpose of emotions?
3. **No. Emotions are not sin.** Feeling them is not the sin. What we do with them can be. And they have a purpose.
4. **Jesus was angry.** He overturned the tables in the temple, and called the Pharisees hypocrites, liars and
   whited sepulchres. Mark 3:5 · Matthew 23:27 · John 8:55. If the sinless Christ felt anger, anger itself cannot be sin.
5. **Your emotions have a reason.** The powerful know something; the foolish lack something. Your mind is passive
   when you don't understand, and active when you know. That is why God gave us a mind to reason.
6. **Anger at evil, compassion for the hurt.**
7. **Love, and what it protects.** Love; jealousy.
8. **Patience holds back. Good anger moves.**
9. **Regret and shame are an audit.**
10. **Hate evil, not people.** Romans 12:9
11. **When emotions go wrong.** Wars, attacks, lives lost, corruption.
12. **Why we need a good God.** The necessity: emotions aren't evil, so there must be a standard; even love can
    drive people to hate.
13. **Where did our moral code come from?** The reality: laws, right and wrong, accountability. God made us in His
    image and likeness and wrote His law on our hearts. Genesis 1:26 · Romans 2:15
14. **Know the Giver of your emotions.**
15. **Our emotions are part of what makes us human. Without them, what would be the difference between us and
    machines?** Next: What do we mean when we call God jealous? Tell me what you think. Follow for part two.
