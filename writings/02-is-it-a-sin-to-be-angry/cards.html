The question

Some teenagers asked me a question.

We see a lot of evil done from anger, hatred, jealousy and so on.

Is it a sin to be angry, or to experience emotions?

If not, what is the purpose of emotions?

@PRINCEANIMAFM
The answer

No.Emotions are not sin.

Feeling them is not the sin. What we do with them can be.

And they have a purpose. Here is what I believe they are for.

@PRINCEANIMAFM
Even Jesus

Jesuswas angry.

He overturned the tables in the temple. He called the Pharisees hypocrites, liars and whited sepulchres.

“He looked round about on them with anger, being grieved for the hardness of their hearts.”

Mark 3:5 · Matthew 23:27 · John 8:55

If the sinless Christ felt anger, anger itself cannot be sin.

@PRINCEANIMAFM
Knowledge

Your emotionshave a reason.

The powerful know something that gives them power. The foolish lack something that could make them wise.

Your mind is passive when you don't understand, and active when you know. That is why God gave us a mind to reason, and to understand our emotions.

@PRINCEANIMAFM
Anger

Anger at evil,compassion for the hurt.

When there is evil around us, we feel anger towards it.

That same heart feels compassion and sympathy for the victims.

@PRINCEANIMAFM
Love

Love, andwhat it protects.

LoveThrough our emotions we feel love, deep care and affection.
JealousyIt comes from love: wanting to protect what is ours and wanting good for ourselves. But it is easily corrupted when it puts others below itself.
@PRINCEANIMAFM
Restraint & drive

Patience holds back.Good anger moves.

PatienceIt keeps us from acting rashly.
A good angerIt gives us the drive and courage to attempt what our reasoning would stop, when something we know is driving it.
@PRINCEANIMAFM
Conscience

Regret and shameare an audit.

RegretIt shows us where we went wrong.
ShameIt makes us question our actions and intentions: what we ought to do, and what we ought not to.
@PRINCEANIMAFM
Hatred

Hate evil,not people.

When we see evil being done, we feel a surge of energy against it that pushes us to act and correct it. Many have fought to overturn great evils in their nations this way.

“Abhor that which is evil; cleave to that which is good.”

Romans 12:9
@PRINCEANIMAFM
The other side

When emotionsgo wrong.

We cannot pretend not to see the evil done through wrong emotions: wars, attacks, lives lost, corrupt people destroying families and governments.

@PRINCEANIMAFM
The necessity

Why we needa good God.

Having emotions isn't evil, so there has to be a standard to strike the balance between good and evil.

Love is so pure and so genuine, yet it can drive people to hate and hurt those who hurt what they love.

This is why we need a good God.

@PRINCEANIMAFM
The reality

Where did ourmoral code come from?

  • How did our laws come about?
  • How do we tell right from wrong?
  • How do we hold ourselves accountable?

We can tell right from wrong because of the absolute existence of a God who made us in His image and likeness, and wrote His law on our hearts.

“Let us make man in our image, after our likeness.”

Genesis 1:26 · Romans 2:15

The answer

Know the Giverof your emotions.

So how do we know what to do with our emotions?

By knowing the One who gave them: knowing Him as a person, and His will concerning them. That is the knowledge that governs our emotions.

@PRINCEANIMAFM

Our emotions are part of what makes us human.

Without them, what would be the difference between us and machines?
