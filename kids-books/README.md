Four print-ready paperback books, each with its own interior PDF and full-wrap cover PDF.

characters, no clip art, no AI images. That means **no copyright problems**, and on
KDP's AI question you can honestly answer **"No"**.

## Why these four: the market research

- The **Children's Activity Books** category (mazes, dot-to-dot, tracing, word search) is one
  of the biggest on Amazon. "Kids activity books" gets about 90,000 searches a month, and
  about 40% of purchases are gifts.
- Amazon's bestseller lists for children's activity, maze and coloring books are dominated
  by **age-labelled titles** ("Ages 4-8", "Ages 3-5") with **big, bold pages**, an
  **easy-to-hard progression**, an **answer key** and a **certificate**. All four books follow
  that pattern.
- By content type, sales split roughly into coloring (30%), puzzles like mazes and word
  searches (25%), drawing and creative (20%), educational/tracing (15%), and games (10%).
  These four books cover the top categories.
- Most successful KDP coloring and activity books have 50-120 pages and sell for
  $7.99-$9.99. Up to 108 pages, KDP charges the same print cost ($2.30).
- Avoid anything with brand characters (Disney, Pokémon, Bluey, Minecraft and so on). Amazon
  removes those books and can close your account.

## How to publish (about 20 minutes per book)

1. Go to **kdp.amazon.com** and sign in with your Amazon account. Then fill in
   **Account → Tax information** and **Getting paid** (bank details). You can't publish until
   both are complete.
2. Click **Create → Paperback**.
3. **Details page:** copy the title, subtitle, description and keywords for the book from
   the listing kit below. Author: your name.
   Reading age: use the book's age range.
4. **Content page:**
   - ISBN: **Get a free KDP ISBN**.
   - Print options: **Black & white interior, White paper**, trim **8.5 x 11 in**,
     Bleed: **No bleed**, Cover finish: **Matte**.
   - Upload `interior.pdf` as the manuscript and upload `cover.pdf` as the cover
     ("Upload a cover you already have").
   - AI-generated content: **No**.
   - Click **Launch Previewer** and approve it.
5. **Pricing page:** choose Amazon.com as the primary marketplace and enter the suggested
   price. Leave Expanded Distribution off (it pays much less). Click **Publish**.
6. Review takes 3-7 days. When the book is live, order one **author copy** at print cost to
   check print quality.

**Before you publish:** the covers and copyright pages say "Prince Asamoah". To use a
different pen name, change `AUTHOR` in `generator/common.py` and rebuild
(`pip install reportlab pypdfium2 pillow && python generator/build.py`).

**Tip:** the cover size depends on the page count. If you change the number of pages, run
`build.py` again so the spine width is recalculated.

## Next steps to grow sales
- **Series:** make "Book 2" of whichever book sells best. Change the `seed` values and
  `THEMES`/`LEVELS` in the generators to get new, unique puzzles in seconds.
- **Seasonal editions** sell well before holidays (Christmas, Halloween, Easter, summer
  travel). Reuse the word search generator with seasonal word lists.
- **Amazon Ads:** once a book has a few reviews, start a small ($5/day) automatic Sponsored
  Products campaign.
- **Etsy / Gumroad:** you can also sell the `interior.pdf` files as printable downloads
  ($3-5 each).
