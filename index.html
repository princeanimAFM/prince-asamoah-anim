About

Two disciplines, one habit: paying attention

Nursing taught me to read people and numbers at the same time: observations, trends, the small change that matters before it becomes a big one.

I began in a busy Accident & Emergency unit in Ghana, doing triage, ECGs and IV therapy under pressure, then brought that experience to the UK, where I now care for residents in regulated nursing homes in Scotland.

Data science gives me the tools to do that at scale. In January 2026 I completed the 10Alytics Master Data Scientist programme, covering statistics, Excel, Tableau, SQL, Python, exploratory data analysis, machine learning and computer vision.

I’m most interested in where the two meet: clearer dashboards for clinical teams, better use of routine data, and technology that gives people back time to care.
