The demo app is "Sam's Shipping", a made-up business with sample data and no country, marked DEMO on every screen.

## Files

## Voice-over script (for reading over the no-voice version)
Read at a calm, friendly pace. Each line starts at the time shown, when the screen changes.

| Starts at | What's on screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | "Run a shipping business?" | Do you run a shipping business, and spend your whole day chasing payments? |
| 0:05 | Customer messages piling up, notebook | Customers asking, "where's my parcel?" Payment screenshots everywhere. And everything written in a notebook. |
| 0:11 | The app opens: list of parcels | Here's a simple app built for exactly that. Every parcel, every customer, and who still owes you, all on one screen. |
| 0:19 | Tapping "Mark as arrived at port" | When the ship lands, tap once, and the customer gets a message straight away. |
| 0:24 | Customer's tracking page | They can check their own tracking link, any time. No more calls. |
| 0:29 | "Who hasn't paid" list, reminders sent | Who hasn't paid? Send them all a reminder, in one tap. |
| 0:34 | "Want an app like this?" end screen | Want an app like this for your business? I'm RMR Dev Works. Send me a message, or email me. It's in my bio. |

### Recording it in TikTok
1. Upload `rmr-video-1-shipping-no-voice.mp4`.
2. Tap **Voiceover** (on the editing screen) and record while the video plays. Start each line when the screen changes.
3. Or record on the phone's voice recorder in a quiet room, then send it to Claude to put into the video.
