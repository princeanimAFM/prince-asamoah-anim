# Barber and salon video, full version (RMR Dev Works)

Vertical 1080×1920, AI voice. Polished style, broad hook, reflective ending.
The demo app is "Corner Chair Barbers", a made-up barbershop with sample data and no country, marked DEMO. The
"This week" numbers are examples.

## Voice-over script
| Segment | What's on screen | Said |
|---|---|---|
| hook | Opening | This one link ends booking chaos for barbers and salons. Watch what it does. |
| pain | A busy Saturday: messages, missed calls, a messy note | Think about a busy Saturday. Are you free at three? Can you fit me in? Two people turn up at the same time, and another never shows up at all. You're halfway through a cut, clippers in one hand and your phone buzzing in the other. Bookings in a notebook, in your messages, and in your head. |
| intro | The booking page | Now picture this. A booking page with your name on it. Your services, your prices, and every free time, all on one link. |
| share | Link and QR code | Put that link in your bio, on your status, or as a code on a card at the chair. Customers book themselves, at any hour, even while you're asleep. |
| pick | Choosing a service and a barber | They choose a service, and who they want to cut it. A skin fade, with Jo. |
| time | Free and taken time slots | Then they only see the times you are really free. Taken slots are greyed out, so double bookings can't happen. |
| confirm | Booked, with a confirmation | One tap, and it's booked. They get a confirmation with the time and the place, and a button to add it to their calendar. |
| remind | Reminder on the customer's phone | The day before, and again an hour before, the app sends them a reminder. They can confirm, or move it with one tap. That alone means far fewer no-shows. |
| day | The barber's day on one screen | Your day sits on one screen. Who is in the chair, who is next, and the gaps in between. Tap when they arrive. Tap again when you're done. |
| cancel | A cancelled slot reopens and is filled | Someone cancels? That time opens up again straight away, and the next person can take it. No more empty chair. |
| regulars | A regular rebooks in one tap | Your regulars rebook in one tap, because the app remembers their usual cut, and who they like to see. |
| stats | Cuts, no-shows, busiest days | At the end of the week, you see how many cuts you did, how many people didn't show, and your busiest days. So you know when to open longer. |
| addup | The week added up | Now add it up. No double bookings. No more, are you free, messages. Far fewer no-shows. And a full diary, even while you're busy cutting. |
| reflect | The reflection | You became a barber to make people look and feel sharp, not to chase messages. Let the app run the diary, and you get back to the chair. |
| cta | The ending | If your business could use time back like this, I'm RMR Dev Works. Send me a message, or email me. It's in my bio. |
