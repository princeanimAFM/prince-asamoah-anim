# Café and coffee shop video, full version (RMR Dev Works)

Vertical 1080×1920, AI voice. Polished style, broad hook, reflective ending.
The demo app is "Little Cup Coffee", a made-up café with sample data and no country, marked DEMO. The numbers
on the "Today" and "This week" screens are examples.

## Voice-over script
| Segment | What's on screen | Said |
|---|---|---|
| hook | Opening | This one link ends the morning coffee queue. Watch what it does. |
| pain | The morning rush: messages, missed calls, a lost stamp card | Think about the morning rush. A queue out of the door. Oat milk, no sugar, extra shot, or was it decaf? Orders shouted over the machine, a paper stamp card nobody can find, and customers who give up and walk away. |
| intro | The menu on one link | Now picture this. Your whole menu on one link. Customers open it in the queue, or on their way in, and order from their phone. |
| share | Link and QR code for the counter and tables | Put the code on the counter, on every table, and in your bio. They scan it, and they're ordering. |
| custom | Choosing oat milk and an extra shot | They choose their drink, then make it theirs. Oat milk, an extra shot. It all goes on the order, written clearly, so nobody has to shout over the machine. |
| cart | Adding a bun, a name for the cup and a pickup time; sending | They add a cinnamon bun, give a name for the cup, and pick a pickup time. The total adds itself up. One tap, and the order is sent. |
| board | The bar screen: new, brewing, ready | In the café, every order lands on one screen, in the order it came in, with the milk and the extras clear. Tap once when it's brewing. Tap again when it's ready. |
| ready | The customer's phone: order ready | And the customer is told the moment it's ready, so nobody stands at the counter wondering. |
| busy | Busy mode: +10 min on every order | Rush too heavy? Switch on busy mode. Every order now shows a ten minute wait, in one tap, so nobody waits longer than you promised. |
| soldout | Switching off oat milk | Out of oat milk, or down to the last cinnamon bun? Switch it off, and it disappears from the menu. |
| loyalty | The stamp card fills; the tenth is free | And the stamp card that always gets lost? It lives in their phone now. Every coffee adds a stamp, and the tenth one is free. |
| regulars | A regular reorders in one tap | Your regulars order their usual in one tap. Flat white, oat milk, extra shot. Done. |
| day | Orders by hour | At the end of the day, you see what sold, and exactly when the rush hits. So you put more hands on in the busy hours, and bake what people actually want. |
| addup | The week added up | Now add it up. A shorter queue. No wrong orders. No lost stamp cards. And customers who come back, because ordering from you is easy. |
| reflect | The reflection | You opened a café to bring people together over a good cup, not to shout orders over a machine. Let the app take the orders, and you get back to the coffee. |
| cta | The ending | If your business could use time back like this, I'm RMR Dev Works. Send me a message, or email me. It's in my bio. |
