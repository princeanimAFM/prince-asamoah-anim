Post it once @rmrdevworks has at least one video up, so people who tap through find something.

## Option A: the ready-made clip (17 seconds)
File: `rmr-announcement-no-voice.mp4`. Text and logo only, so add a sound in TikTok:
1. Switch to the **personal** account, tap **+**, **Upload**, choose the clip, **Next**.
2. Tap **Add sound** and pick an upbeat trending sound. The personal account can use any song.
3. Tap **Next**, paste the caption below, and type the @rmrdevworks tag so it becomes a link.
4. Post between 7 and 9 pm.
