# Church app video: voice-over script

About 2½ minutes. For a calm, warm voice: read it as if you're gently telling a church leader how much easier
their week could be, with care rather than as an advert.

## How to record
1. Use the phone's voice recorder (Voice Memos on iPhone, Recorder on Android), in a quiet room with soft
   furnishings, such as a bedroom with curtains closed.
2. Hold the phone a hand's width from your mouth, slightly to the side.
3. **Record everything in one take.** Between each numbered section, **stay silent for about 2 seconds.** That
   pause is how the video knows where each scene starts. Don't read the numbers or titles out loud.
4. Small slips are fine, so just keep going. If a section goes badly wrong, stop and start the whole recording
   again (it only takes about 3 minutes).
5. Take your time. If you speak slower than the guide video, the scenes simply stay on screen longer.
6. Send the recording to Prince, who sends it to Claude.

## The script

### 1. Opening

*Warm and inviting, like you're about to tell a friend something important.*

> If your church still runs on group chats and paper notes, this video is for you. Stay to the end, because the last part matters most.

### 2. A normal week

*Slower and honest. Let each line land. A small pause after each sentence.*

> Think about a normal week. The pastor, up late, copying the same announcement into five group chats. Visitors who came once, and never heard from anyone again. Prayer requests on scraps of paper. And members who quietly stopped coming, without anyone noticing.

### 3. One home

*Brighter. This is the turn: hope.*

> Now picture one home for the whole church, on every member's phone. Services, sermons, the Bible, events and prayer, all in one place, with your church's name on it.

### 4. Announcements

> Post an announcement once, and every member gets a notification, in their own time zone. No more copying and pasting. No more, "I didn't see the message."

### 5. Sermons and live services

> Every sermon lands in the app by itself. Anyone who was sick, travelling or working on Sunday can still hear the word that same day. And you can stream your service live, so nobody is left out.

### 6. Verse of the day

> Every morning, a verse of the day arrives on their phone, so your members start the day in the word, even between Sundays.

### 7. Prayer requests

> Prayer requests come in privately, straight to the prayer team. Each one is seen, prayed over and marked, so nothing gets lost in a pocket.

### 8. First-time visitors

> When someone visits for the first time, they fill in a short welcome card, and a leader is reminded to follow up within two days. Every visitor feels wanted, and many come back.

### 9. Member care (the heart of it)

*Slow down here. This is the most important part.*

> And this is the part that matters most. The app shows who hasn't been seen for three weeks, so a leader can reach out before they drift away, while there's still time.

### 10. Events

> For events, members tap to say they're coming, so you know how many chairs and how much food you need, before the day.

### 11. Serving teams

> Serving teams see their rota in the app, and get a reminder the day before. No more last minute gaps on a Sunday morning.

### 12. Add it up

> Now add it up. Hours of typing and chasing, given back every single week. Visitors followed up. Members cared for, before they slip away.

### 13. The reflection

*Slowest and most gentle of all. Soft, sincere, unhurried.*

> Every name on that care list is a person God has placed in your hands. An app can't love them for you. But it can make sure none of them is ever forgotten, and give your pastor time for people, not a phone.

### 14. The ending

*Warm and friendly. Say R-M-R as three letters: "R. M. R. Dev Works".*

> If your church could use time back like this, I'm RMR Dev Works. Send me a message, or email me. It's in my bio.
